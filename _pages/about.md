Welcome! I’m a researcher in computational soft-matter physics. My work bridges the gap between theoretical models and large-scale computation, primarily focusing on the non-equilibrium dynamics of active systems through GPU-accelerated Molecular Dynamics (MD) simulations. Beyond the simulations, I spend much of my time reading science fiction, exploring its philosophical dimensions and the broader role scientific literature plays in shaping society.
